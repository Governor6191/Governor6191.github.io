Optimal routing of accident victims: ACO, ALNS, and the Kumasi road network.

Optimization EMS routing Network

Ambulance dispatch in resource-constrained cities like Kumasi is bottlenecked less by distance than by hospital capability — a Level-1 trauma case routed to the nearest facility may still need a costly transfer. This project formulates dispatch as a multi-objective routing problem on the real Kumasi road network and benchmarks Ant Colony Optimization against Adaptive Large Neighborhood Search, two-stage stochastic programming, deep reinforcement learning, and a hybrid ACO+ML pipeline. It extends my undergraduate thesis at KNUST (2023), which used ACO alone.

Shock-wave formation in traffic flow via the LWR conservation law.

Numerical PDE Traffic flow Hyperbolic

A first-order Godunov finite-volume scheme for the Lighthill–Whitham–Richards scalar conservation law, validated against vehicle trajectories from the NGSIM US-101 dataset. The aim is to characterize shock formation and rarefaction structure under realistic fundamental-diagram fits and to quantify model fidelity in regimes where the macroscopic continuum assumption is most strained. Started as a term project for MA 8213 (Applied Mathematics II) and is being extended into a working paper.

Spatial analysis of typhoid fever in Ghana.

Spatial statistics Public health Hotspot analysis

A six-chapter analytical report on the spatial distribution of typhoid fever cases across regions of Ghana. Used choropleth mapping and hotspot/spatial-autocorrelation analysis to identify geographic clusters and surface candidate drivers for further investigation. The final manuscript carries a 42-entry bibliography. Not currently slated for publication; available on request.
