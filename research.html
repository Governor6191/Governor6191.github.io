Optimal routing of accident victims: ACO, ALNS, and the Kumasi road network.

Optimization EMS routing Network

Ambulance dispatch in resource-constrained cities like Kumasi is bottlenecked less by distance than by hospital capability. A Level-1 trauma case routed to the nearest facility may still need a costly secondary transfer, and the routing-versus-capability trade-off is a systematic source of preventable harm. This project formulates dispatch as a multi-objective routing problem on the real Kumasi road network and benchmarks Ant Colony Optimization against ALNS, two-stage stochastic programming, deep reinforcement learning, and a hybrid ACO+ML pipeline. It extends my undergraduate thesis at KNUST (2023), which used ACO alone. A US Gulf Coast extension, applying the same framework to hurricane-response dispatch, is planned.

Shock-wave formation in traffic flow via the LWR conservation law.

Numerical PDE Traffic flow Hyperbolic

A first-order Godunov finite-volume scheme for the Lighthill-Whitham-Richards scalar conservation law, calibrated and validated against vehicle trajectories from the NGSIM US-101 dataset. Predicted shock speeds match observed backward-propagating congestion waves in direction and order of magnitude. Completed as a term paper for MA 8213 (Applied Mathematics II), Spring 2026. Currently being extended into a working paper.
