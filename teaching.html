How I think about it

Numerical methods, in particular, fail in ways that are diagnostic. A diverging iterate isn't a mystery. It's the algorithm telling you which assumption you've over-trusted.

I try to push students past the syntax of a method and into the habit of asking what each step is buying them and what it costs. The students I learn most from are the ones who push back; the courses I teach best are the ones I'm still slightly afraid of.
