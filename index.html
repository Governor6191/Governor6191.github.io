Numerical PDE Traffic Flow Course paper · v1 complete

Shock-wave formation & the LWR conservation law.

A first-order Godunov finite-volume scheme for the Lighthill–Whitham–Richards scalar conservation law, calibrated and validated against vehicle trajectories from the NGSIM US-101 dataset. Predicted shock speeds match observed backward-propagating congestion waves in direction and order of magnitude; the report runs 37 pages with 49 equations and four figures.
